1. Introduction

Welcome to Rivika, a jewellery catalogue application developed and maintained by RTD CREATIVES. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and delete information when you use the Rivika Android application.

Rivika allows users to explore silver jewellery collections, view product information, and save their favourite jewellery designs.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited information necessary to provide our application's features and services.

Personal information

  • Name: To identify and personalize your account.
  • Email address: For account registration, authentication, and account-related communication.
  • User ID: A unique identifier generated by Firebase Authentication to manage your account.

Wishlist information

We store jewellery products that you save to your wishlist so that you can access your favourite designs when using your account.

Technical information

Our Firebase service providers may process certain technical information, such as authentication identifiers, device information, and service diagnostics, to operate and secure the application.

Rivika does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, microphone recordings, or financial payment information through its stated catalogue features.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users securely.
  • Display jewellery collections and product details.
  • Save and synchronize wishlist information.
  • Personalize the application experience.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.

We do not sell or rent personal information to advertisers or third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Rivika uses the following Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication: Used to register users and manage email-based authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore: Used to store account-related information and wishlist data.
  • Firebase Cloud Storage: Used to host and deliver jewellery catalogue images and other application content.

These services may process information necessary for their operation. For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy Information.

5. Advertising

Rivika does not display third-party advertisements. We do not use advertising networks to serve personalized advertisements within the application.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information. Information may be processed by Google Firebase as our service provider to operate authentication, database, and storage functionality. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted communication with Firebase services, secure authentication mechanisms, database access controls, and restricted access to account information.

However, no electronic storage or internet transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain account information and wishlist data for as long as your account remains active or as necessary to provide application services.

When you request account deletion, we remove your account information and associated wishlist data within 7 working days after verifying the request. Certain information may be retained for a longer period where required by applicable law or legitimate security obligations. Such retention will be limited to what is necessary.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request permanent deletion of your Rivika account and associated personal data at any time.

  1. Send an email to rivikajewels@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Delete Account.
  3. Send the request from your registered email address.
  4. Our team will verify your request and process the deletion.

Any legally required retained information will be handled in accordance with applicable laws.

10. Children's Privacy

Rivika is a general jewellery catalogue application and is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate account details, deletion of your account and associated information, or clarification about our privacy practices. Please send requests to rivikajewels@gmail.com.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

RTD CREATIVES may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, application functionality, or legal obligations. Updates will be published on this page, and the effective date will be revised accordingly.
